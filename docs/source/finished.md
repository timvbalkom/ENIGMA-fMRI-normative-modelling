## Quality control
Instructions on quality control, with examples of good and bad quality, are reported in [the HALFpipe manual](https://fmri.science/halfpipe/quality-control.html). Please let us know if you experience any problems with quality control that we may be able to assist in.

Please save the results of your quality assessment. There are two potential methods:
- You can use the **Export** function on the `index.html` quality reports. However, please note that if you do not export your ratings, the ratings may be lost after the session, e.g., after cookies are deleted.
- An alternative is to register the quality assessment using an Excel sheet. You can find a sample quality assessment Excel sheet [here](https://surfdrive.surf.nl/s/wGg2MsPXPsCT5ym).

Please also register and share the **framewise displacement (FD)**, **DVARS** and other potential image quality measures for your subjects, as these will be used in the analyses.

## Quality control
Instructions on quality control, with examples of good and bad quality, are reported in [the HALFpipe manual](https://fmri.science/halfpipe/quality-control.html). Please let us know if you experience any problems with quality control that we may be able to assist in.

Please register the framewise displacement (FD), DVARS and other potential image quality measures for your subjects, as these will be used in the analyses. You can find a sample quality assessment Excel sheet [here]().
